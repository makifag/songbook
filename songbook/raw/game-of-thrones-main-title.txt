As a fan of the Show I love it's opening music. I'm into finger picking lately so after 
a while I decided to get to work and to arrange the music for solo guitar. So here you go.

Comments and feedback are really appreciated also I'm accepting Donations if you like 
what I do and want to encourage me to take more time to make more
arrangement you can do so following this link http://tinyurl.com/ckajzfx.
