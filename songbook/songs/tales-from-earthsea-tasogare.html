Legend
---------------
W - whole duration
H - half
Q - quarter
E - 8th
S - 16th
s - staccato (means "play then kill the sustain of note" )
. - note dotted
|-n-|  - n-tuplets
L - tied note
x - don't touch chord but hit the table guitar wood with your thumb ,then play the next note (the E bass)
(n) - ghost note
NH - natural harmonic
h - hammer on
p - pull off
~ - vibrato
^ - brush up
